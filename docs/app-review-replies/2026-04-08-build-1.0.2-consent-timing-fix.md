# App Review Reply — Build 1.0.2 (7)

**Date:** 2026-04-08
**Submission ID:** 84a5688d-3853-4b29-b948-1e2f77c2b96c
**Build:** 1.0.2 (7)
**Addresses:** Guidelines 5.1.1(i) / 5.1.2(i) — consent disclosure timing for the Record Your Chanting feature

## Context

Apple's second rejection cited that the consent disclosure appeared *after* voice data had been captured, rather than before. The fix in build 1.0.2 (7) moves the consent gate from the Stop Recording branch to the Start Recording branch, so the modal appears before the microphone is activated.

## Reply (paste into App Store Connect)

> Hello,
>
> Thank you for the detailed feedback on the previous submission. We have addressed the issue in build **1.0.2 (7)**, now available in App Store Connect.
>
> **Fix for Guidelines 5.1.1(i) / 5.1.2(i)**
>
> We understand the issue: in the previous build, the in-app consent disclosure for the Record Your Chanting feature appeared after the user tapped Stop Recording. This meant that audio had been captured locally before the disclosure was shown, even though no data was transmitted to Sarvam AI before consent.
>
> In build 1.0.2 (7), the consent disclosure now appears the moment the user taps **Record Your Chanting**, *before* the microphone is activated. No audio is captured until the user has explicitly tapped **Allow** on the disclosure. The same disclosure already appears before the **Listen** feature contacts Sarvam AI for text-to-speech, and that flow is unchanged.
>
> The disclosure clearly identifies:
>
> - **Sarvam AI** as a third-party AI service based in India
> - **Data sent:** the Sanskrit verse text (for audio generation) and the user's voice recording (for pronunciation scoring)
> - **Data not sent:** no name, account information, contacts, location, or other personal data
> - **Retention:** audio is not stored after Sarvam AI processes the request
> - Links to both Sarvam AI's privacy policy and GitaFlow's privacy policy
>
> Consent is persisted only after the user explicitly grants it, and can be revoked at any time from **Settings → Privacy → Revoke Consent**, after which the next attempt to use either feature will re-prompt the user before any data is captured or transmitted.
>
> Our privacy policy at https://gist.github.com/gautamdn/e69083c28914e5839cdbc19bc6f66575 identifies what data is collected, how it is collected, all uses of that data, and the in-app consent flow, as required by Guideline 5.1.1(i).
>
> **Test credentials**
>
> The pronunciation features (Listen, Record Your Chanting) require a Sarvam AI API key. A test key has been provided in the **App Review Information → Notes** section of this submission.
>
> **Steps to verify in build 1.0.2 (7):**
>
> 1. Install the app on a fresh device or delete the previous version.
> 2. Open the app → tap the gear icon (Settings) → scroll to "Audio & AI (Sarvam AI)".
> 3. Paste the test API key from App Review Notes into the field and tap **Save Key**.
> 4. Open Practice (or any verse → Practice).
> 5. Tap **Record Your Chanting**. The Sarvam AI consent disclosure appears immediately, *before* the recording indicator. No audio is being captured at this point.
> 6. Tap **Allow**. Only now does the recording begin and the persistent red recording banner appear at the top of the screen.
> 7. Speak briefly and tap **Stop Recording**. The audio is transcribed by Sarvam AI and a pronunciation score is displayed.
> 8. To verify revocation: Settings → Privacy → **Revoke Consent**. Return to Practice and tap Record Your Chanting. The disclosure re-appears before any audio is captured.
>
> The same fix applies to the Listen feature, which gates Sarvam AI calls behind the same consent disclosure on the Reading, Practice, Memorize, and Ask Gita screens.
