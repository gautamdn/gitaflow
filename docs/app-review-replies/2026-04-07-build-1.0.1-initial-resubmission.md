## Context

Apple's original rejection of build 1.0 cited two issues:

1. **5.1.1(i) / 5.1.2(i)** — App sent data to Sarvam AI without in-app disclosure or consent.
2. **2.5.14** — Recording feature lacked a clear visual indicator that audio was being recorded.

Build 1.0.1 (6) added the in-app consent gate, the persistent recording banner, and updated the privacy policy.

## Reply (paste into App Store Connect)

> Hello,
>
> Thank you for the detailed review. We have addressed both issues in build **1.0.1 (6)**, now available in App Store Connect.
>
> **1. Guidelines 5.1.1(i) / 5.1.2(i) — Privacy / Data Collection & Use**
>
> GitaFlow now displays a full-screen consent modal the first time the user attempts to use any feature that contacts Sarvam AI. The modal:
>
> - Identifies **Sarvam AI** by name as a third-party AI service based in India
> - Explains exactly what data is sent: the **Sanskrit verse text** (for audio generation) and the user's **voice recording** (for pronunciation scoring)
> - Explicitly lists what is NOT sent: no name, no account, no contacts, no location, no other personal data
> - Links to both Sarvam AI's privacy policy and our own
> - Requires the user to tap **Allow** before any data is sent. Tapping **Not Now** cancels the action and no data leaves the device
>
> Consent can be revoked at any time from **Settings → Privacy → Revoke Consent**, after which the next Sarvam AI call will re-prompt for consent.
>
> Our privacy policy has been updated to describe this in-app consent flow, what data is sent to Sarvam AI, and the user's right to revoke.
>
> **2. Guideline 2.5.14 — Recording indicators**
>
> While the Practice screen is recording audio, a persistent red banner is displayed at the top of the screen containing a pulsing white dot, the word "Recording", and a live elapsed-time counter (mm:ss). The banner cannot be dismissed by the user and remains visible the entire time recording is active, regardless of scroll position. It disappears only when recording stops.
>
> **Steps to reproduce on the new build:**
>
> 1. Open the app (fresh install). Tap any verse, then tap **Listen** → the consent modal appears before any data is sent.
> 2. Tap **Allow**, then go to Practice → tap **Record Your Chanting** → the persistent red recording banner appears at the top of the screen.
> 3. Settings → Privacy → **Revoke Consent** → repeat step 1 to verify the gate re-prompts.
>
> Thank you for your patience.
>
> — GitaFlow team
