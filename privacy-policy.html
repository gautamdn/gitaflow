GitaFlow — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 7, 2026

Overview

GitaFlow is a Bhagavad Gita study and Sanskrit pronunciation practice app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.
  • We do not require account creation or login.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location, or any data beyond what is described below.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and never transmitted to our servers:

  • Reading progress: Which daily readings you have completed, your current day, and streak count.
  • Pronunciation scores: Your best scores for each shloka you have practiced.
  • App settings: Your preferences for dark mode, font size, and language display options.
  • Sarvam AI API key: If you choose to use the pronunciation practice feature, your API key is stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

The pronunciation practice feature uses Sarvam AI APIs for text-to-speech and speech-to-text processing:

  • Audio recordings are sent to Sarvam AI's servers for transcription and are not stored permanently.
  • Sanskrit text is sent to Sarvam AI for audio generation.
  • Please refer to Sarvam AI's Privacy Policy for their data handling practices.

Before any text or audio is sent to Sarvam AI, GitaFlow asks for your explicit in-app consent. You can revoke this consent at any time from the Settings screen. Use of the pronunciation feature is optional — the core reading experience works entirely offline.

Microphone Access

The app requests microphone access only for the pronunciation practice feature. Audio is recorded temporarily, sent to Sarvam AI for transcription, and then discarded. Recordings are not stored on your device or our servers.

Children's Privacy

GitaFlow is suitable for all ages (rated 4+). We do not knowingly collect any data from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page with an updated effective date.
